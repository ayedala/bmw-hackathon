## Customer studies

### CN_EU_2025

Rows 1–3 hold hierarchical headers. China is B:C; EU is D:F. B = G68, C = G70, D = F70, E = G60, F = G70. A contains category headers on odd rows 3–75 and `Mean` on even rows 4–76. This yields **37 categories × 5 scopes = 185 mean cells**. Columns G:M and trailing rows inflate the used range and are not additional observations.

Examples: G60 EU overall satisfaction is `E4`; G60 EU energy efficiency is `E42`; China G68 rear roominess is `B56`. Preserve raw means. The instrument endpoints and respondent counts are not supplied. Do not assume a confirmed 0–10 scale, compute sampling confidence intervals, or equate mean gaps with statistical significance. Within-instrument descriptive comparisons are possible with explicit limitations.

### US_2025

Column A holds category or response labels; B holds `% of column, sample base`; C is G60 and D is G70. “Sample total” = 1 indicates a proportion base, **not one respondent**. Each category has seven response shares, from `I Hate It` to `I Love It`.

There are **103 category blocks × 2 models = 206 distributions**. All inspected distributions sum to 1 within tolerance 0.005; seven categories occur in each. The tolerance is an inspection choice, not a claim about measurement error. Blank rows near later blocks mean a fixed nine-row stepping parser is unsafe. Detect category headers semantically and validate the seven labels.

Example: `C3:C5` sums to 0.019 for the first category's bottom three responses; do not call that a failure probability across all owners. Retain the distribution instead of converting it to an assumed equal-interval mean. Any crosswalk to CN/EU categories needs an explicit mapping and comparability review.

## Equipment PDFs and briefing

The G60 cover says July 2026; page 2 states edition 2 dated 22 June 2026. Page 8 is a matrix of standard features by derivative: the position of each square matters. A flattened text line cannot reliably identify which derivative has four-zone climate control. A blank in a **selected standard equipment** table does not prove the feature is unavailable as an option.

Use page/table coordinates, column headers, option code, symbol legend, price/tax/currency, market, date and footnotes. Validate a few relevant tables visually first. For the MVP, manual verification of 10–20 relevant equipment facts is acceptable if labelled `manual_verified`; pretending full PDF table automation worked is not.

The briefing text extraction returned empty for both pages. They were rendered and read visually. Treat an empty extract as a parser issue, never as an empty document. Option-list text is available but has spacing and column-flattening artifacts.

## Cleaning pipeline and reconciliation

1. Check extension and signature, archive path safety, file size, and hashes. Keep sources immutable.
2. Read workbooks without evaluating formulas or macros. Validate expected headers; propose mappings for unfamiliar schemas and request confirmation only for ambiguous semantics.
3. Group annotations by `file hash + ID`; verify text and metadata consistency; quarantine conflicts.
4. Normalize whitespace in a separate field. Keep raw quote offsets or a reversible raw-to-clean span map. Remove boilerplate survey prompts only with logged rules.
5. Identify and redact names/contact data before model submission or public demo. Do not derive expertise from a person's writing style. Retain private original evidence locally.
6. Preserve unknown/null/not-applicable/zero distinctly. Keep legacy labels with their origin; exclude them from blind extraction prompts.
7. Parse study blocks and sales into long-form records. Every normalized value retains cell address and source hash.
8. Parse equipment tables with a scope whitelist and human verification. Quarantine ambiguous symbols or column mappings.
9. Write reconciliation: accepted source records + quarantined source records = input source records, with annotation rows counted separately. Report every drop and why.
10. Freeze a dataset manifest and split before model tuning. Group every repeated ID and probable repost family into one evaluation split.

## Missing inputs and immediate consequences

No verified source identities → no expert/owner weighting for A–D. No feedback timestamps → no temporal trend claims. No study sample sizes → no survey standard errors. No engineering cost/constraints → feasibility and cost remain pending. No CN verbatims → no Chinese comment prevalence. No trim of feedback vehicle → equipment mismatch cannot be resolved from the option list alone. No adjudicated requirements → no measured prioritization accuracy yet.
